# Adaptive numerical check

The complete primary anchor and B1 comparison selected the same stripe and checkerboard final patterns. The observed change in the stripe versus explicit NM gap on increasing the basis crossed the prespecified 2 meV/Fe target, even though its sign remained unchanged. This triggered B2, a matched stripe and NM pair at 106 Ry and four times this density cutoff, with the primary mesh, width, structural coordinates and tolerance. This cutoff corresponds to the high Fe library guideline. Its purpose is to assess the change from the 90 Ry pair. It is a separate post-observation check and does not overwrite the predeclared primary scan.

The matched S1 stripe–NM comparison changes the same-mesh K1 gap by more than the 2 meV/Fe target. This triggered S2, reducing the cold-smearing width to 0.006 Ry at the same dense mesh and basis with the same converged K1 parents. The observed changes describe finite-width sensitivity at that mesh. Smaller smearing demanding a finer mesh remains a separate numerical consideration. The 0.006 Ry pair was chosen after observing the S1 variation and is not part of the frozen primary discovery grid.

The K1 stripe–checkerboard gap shift was marginally over 2 meV/Fe. K2 therefore increases the in-plane sampling to 8 × 8 while keeping the K1 out-of-plane grid and all other converged-comparison settings. Both branches use only the corresponding own-lane K1 charge density for startup. QE recreates orbitals for the new mesh. This tests further in-plane mesh sensitivity at the original cold width. It does not jointly extrapolate the mesh and smearing to their limits.
