## Geometry and hypotheses

Use the conventional tetragonal P4/nmm origin with Fe at (3/4,1/4,0) and (1/4,3/4,0), and Se at (1/4,1/4,+z) and (3/4,3/4,−z). The translation group of the common four-Fe cell is generated by A+B, −A+B and C, with a single Fe layer per repeat. In this cell both nearest-neighbor directions are half lattice vectors and both AFM patterns fit. The two cosets used to populate the cell are the primitive origin and A. Fitting all initial states in one identical supercell makes the underlying Brillouin-zone quadrature and basis-volume conventions comparable, even if QE finds different symmetry reductions. The cell excludes c-axis staggered layers and other larger magnetic periods. The energy comparison addresses these initial seeds under the stated collinear fixed-geometry model.

Sample a = 3.700, 3.765 and 3.830 Å at fixed c = 5.518 Å and fixed z = 0.2343 (height ≈ 1.293 Å). Keep square in-plane metric and all coordinates fixed. This simple nearly symmetric in-plane strain brackets the source geometry while isolating strain from height response, which the source reports can strongly affect magnetism. It probes discrete points, with narrow refinements only if the evidence justifies them. There is no a–c volume constraint, relaxation, orthorhombicity, vacancy, SOC, dispersion correction, Hubbard U or constrained spin-moment term. The calculation allows a spin seed to collapse. QE's symmetry may maintain symmetries compatible with the type labels. Check QE's weighted site spin signs, global moment and integrated absolute spin polarization to identify retained, collapsed, mixed or unresolved outcomes.

## Numerical plan and criteria

Use Debian quantum-espresso 6.7-3+b1 (actual banner PWSCF 6.7MaX), exact PseudoDojo ONCV PBE scalar-relativistic Fe/Se pair, with 128 valence electrons per common cell. Use MPI k-point pools, a serial OpenBLAS numerical library, PBE, unshifted regular meshes, cold (Marzari–Vanderbilt) smearing and local-TF mixing. The Fe spin labels initialize ±0.22 of the valence spin-polarization fraction with no moment constraint. Use the identical Fe UPF for both type labels. The explicit NM target at the anchor has nspin=1.

Primary scan P1 uses 60/240 Ry, 4×4×3 common-cell mesh, 0.021 Ry cold smearing, mixing 0.40, conv_thr = 1e-7 Ry per cell. The deliberately moderate basis and mesh are a finite-cost primary sampling and have no presupposed adequacy. The common library's recommended plane-wave values are useful as a high-basis check, not an energy-difference certificate. Record the native QE energy and its printed smearing contribution when available. All inter-start comparisons initially use the same energy convention and a shared per-cell divisor of four. Convert from Ry/cell to meV/Fe with 1 Ry = 13605.693122994 meV. Reference each a to the lowest verified result for visualization or to an explicitly documented common branch, and compare the anchor stripe with an explicit NM calculation.

At the anchor, test one factor at a time against P1: basis 90/360 Ry (B1) and mesh 6×6×5 (K1). Evaluate smearing sensitivity at the denser mesh by reducing the width to 0.012 Ry, with the same settings otherwise. Prioritize stripe and its nearest competitor for each check, retaining both comparable energies at each level; when the competitor changes, include it. Target combined resolution of 2 meV/Fe on the primary gap, using summed observed setting-dependent shifts as a conservative diagnostic, not a rigorous error bound. A robustness claim requires stable final orders and sign under checked settings, with a gap exceeding the observed envelope. Test endpoints where energetic separation changes or is marginal and use tighter or combined settings if any single shift approaches the threshold. A representative replay uses an independent clean attempt and a target difference ≤0.2 meV/Fe, with the same final classification and visible SCF convergence.

Strict SCF acceptance requires terminal successful exit, the explicit QE SCF-achieved statement, a final finite energy and an observed final SCF residual estimate within tolerance or a numeric upper bound below tolerance. A stopped or unconverged run is retained but supplies no plotted energy. Classify Fe signs from the last complete site report at convergence. Treat a site magnitude ≤0.05 μB as effectively zero for pattern matching and use a 0.10 μB/Fe integrated-absolute-moment threshold to classify global collapse. The model supports conclusions only to the extent actual setting variations test them. Report unresolved ordering, interpolation, structural assumptions or incomplete convergence work explicitly.
