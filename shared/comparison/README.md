# Cross-track comparison

Keep this area free of cross-track scientific information until both tracks have reached an independent result or a documented stopping point.

At the comparison gate, compare at least:

- scientific correctness and agreement/disagreement of the final result;
- reproducibility and provenance;
- amount and type of human intervention;
- computational cost and throughput;
- convergence/validation discipline;
- failure handling and debugging behavior;
- epistemic reliability: whether the confidence expressed by each track is justified by the evidence it produced.

The comparison is meant to diagnose practical strengths and weaknesses of the two workflows, not to force either track to agree with the other.
